Boat Tail — Structural & Thermal FEA

ANSYS · flown on Anubis

Qualified the aft-most airframe component under 4,000 N peak thrust from an N-class motor. Static Structural (1.2M elements, 0.5 mm refinement at the step shoulder) and a Transient Thermal model (11 load steps, 4 s burn → 120 s soak) drove the geometry. Custom composite part with a 3D-printed core; flew at IREC.

Active Airbrake Control System

SOAR · IREC 2025 / 2026

Designed a servo-actuated active drag system to hit a precise target apogee, with three flaps varying frontal area under closed-loop control. A servo-and-linkage mechanism converts rotational to linear motion; a Simulink lookup-table model commands deployment. Flew as a powered, integrated bay, active in-flight at IREC 2026.

Personal High-Power Rocketry & Avionics

TRA L1/L2 certified

Built and flew multiple high-power rockets, including a TRA L2 vehicle to 2,900 ft on an Aerotech J250W. Hand-soldered a dual-deployment flight computer transmitting live telemetry to a self-built ground station, packaged into the airframe and validated through ejection and vacuum-chamber testing. Flight data matched OpenRocket predictions to under 5%.
