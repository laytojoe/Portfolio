Boat Tail — Structural & Thermal FEA

ANSYS · flown on Anubis

Qualified the aft-most airframe component (motor retainer and base-drag reduction) under 4,000 N peak thrust from an N-class motor, using ANSYS Static Structural and Transient Thermal (4 s burn → 120 s soak). The 243 °C peak stays confined to the bore surface for under a second; the bulk averages ~31 °C and the PPA-CF body never exceeds 39 °C, well below its Tg. Flew at IREC.

Static-Fire Stand — Failure & Redesign

SOAR · propulsion test

An off-nominal SRAD propellant burn pushed peak force past 1,400 lbf and chamber pressure above 800 psi, failing a stand sized for nominal thrust with no margin. Root-caused the failure and redesigned the stand with new materials and structural supports, verified in ANSYS to a factor of safety of 3.2. The new stand has since supported successful N2650 static fires.

Fin Flutter Margin — Modal Analysis

ANSYS · carbon fiber fins

Studied bending/torsion frequency separation to raise flutter margin at Mach 0.85–0.9, sweeping leading-edge angle on a quasi-isotropic carbon fiber plate. Mode 1 bending stayed flat at ~137 Hz while Mode 2 torsion rose 23% (177 → 217 Hz). Selected the 55° sweep, lifting the torsion-to-bending ratio from 1.29 to 1.58 with no bending-stiffness penalty.
